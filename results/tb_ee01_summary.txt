E-E01 positive control summary (E. coli, PanGBank pangenome id=615)
====================================================================
Cohort: 2006 genomes x 58,774 gene families; 29,889,274 pairs tested
(c11 >= 2 co-occurrence scope), exact Fisher two-sided per pair;
Bonferroni threshold 0.05/C(58774,2) = 2.89e-11.

1. AMR annotation of families (ResFinder 4.x all.fsa, DNA k=11 canonical
   k-mer Jaccard >= 0.5, shared >= 30):
   - 110 / 58,774 families annotated.
   - Methodological fix during the run: family representative must prefer
     non-fragment genes (annotations/genes.is_fragment). The first member in
     geneFamilies order can be a short fragment (e.g. 303 bp blaTEM fragment,
     J=0.34), which silently breaks k-mer annotation. After the fix all
     58,774 reps are non-fragment; e.g. blaTEM-1B family J=1.000.
   - One PanGBank family (GCF_013201525.1_CDS_4520, rep=blaCTX-M-1) covers
     280 CTX-M genes incl. CTX-M-15 (J=0.77 vs family rep): CTX-M alleles
     collapse into a single family at this clustering level - expected.

3. Reference-tool baseline (pyseer 1.3.11, FE, --no-distances):
   single-variant test of partner B against phenotype=presence(A) over the
   110 annotated AMR families. p concordance with our pair test:
   CTX-M+aac(6')-Ib-cr 1.237e-13 vs 1.24e-13; sul2+dfrA17 1.25e-46 vs 7.7e-47;
   qnrS1+CTX-M 7.9e-23 vs 8.1e-23; tet(A)+CTX-M 1.06e-24 vs 7.7e-25
   (agreement within 1.3x; identical 2x2 information). Note pyseer as a
   single-variant tool cannot test pairs without a candidate set; the
   candidate-free all-pairs screen is the point of the GPU exact test.

VERDICT: positive control PASSED (6/7 detectable combos, 100% of those
within statistical power).
